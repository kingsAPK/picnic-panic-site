Picnic Panic – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Picnic Panic ("the app") is published by APKINGS (PRIVATE) LIMITED ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

No data collected

The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

Works offline

The game runs entirely on your device and does not need an internet connection. Your game progress (levels, stars, settings) is saved only locally on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
